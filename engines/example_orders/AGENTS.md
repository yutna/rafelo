# Rafelo::ExampleOrders

An example product module. Read the root AGENTS.md first. It follows the app
layering: pages render one screen, containers own hooks and state, components
stay presentational (FE-01), domain/ and forms/ are framework-free (OOP-04),
and all user-facing copy lives in config/locales (CT-08). Use
`example_customers` only through its public surface (ARCH-09).
